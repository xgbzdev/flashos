Welcome to Flash Notepad!

Flash Notepad is a little app for FlashOS. It has five tabs:

  Text        write and read .txt files
  Draw        paint with the mouse and save your drawing as a .bmp picture
  Pictures    look at .jpg .png .gif and .bmp pictures
  Video       watch .flv videos (Sorenson Spark with MP3 sound)
  Calculator  add, subtract, multiply and divide

The File and Edit menus are in the cyan border at the top.

Keyboard shortcuts
  Ctrl+N  new          Ctrl+O  open        Ctrl+S  save
  Ctrl+Z  undo         Ctrl+X / C / V      cut, copy, paste
  Ctrl+A  select all   Ctrl+F  find        F5  time and date
  Ctrl+1 ... Ctrl+5  switch tabs
  F9 / F10 / F11  sound off, quieter, louder
  F12  close Flash Notepad

When FlashOS is installed on the hard disk, everything you save is
kept on the disk, even after you restart. When FlashOS runs from a CD,
your files stay in memory until you restart.

Have fun!
